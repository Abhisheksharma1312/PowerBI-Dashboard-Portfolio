## Sales Performance Dashboard

### Overview
A two-view Power BI dashboard analyzing sales performance for a book retail business operating on Flipkart & Amazon — covering 60+ SKUs across multiple states. Built to give leadership a fast executive read and give operations a row-level drill-down for the same data.

### Business Objective
Leadership needed a single source of truth to answer:
- Are we growing month over month, and where are we losing momentum?
- Which states and products are driving revenue — and which are underperforming?
- Which authors/categories should get more shelf space and ad spend?

### Key Insights Surfaced
- **₹963K** in total sales across **4K orders**, at an average order value of **₹270**
- **Uttar Pradesh** is the top-performing state (**₹96K**), with a long tail across 10 states
- **"The Secret English"** is the single best-selling title (**₹254K** — well ahead of #2)
- **J.K. Rowling** accounts for **52%** of author-driven sales, signaling a concentration risk worth flagging to category managers
- Monthly trend reveals volatility (a steep Jan→Feb drop before recovering in March) worth investigating against marketing spend or seasonality

### Tools & Techniques Used
- **Power BI Desktop** - data modeling, report design
- **Power Query** - data cleaning and shaping
- **DAX** - calculated measures (Total Sales, AOV, YoY/MoM comparisons)
- **Data modeling** - star-schema relationships across product, state, and time dimensions
- Interactive slicers (Event Type, State, Month, Year) for self-serve filtering

### What This Project Demonstrates
- Designing for **two audiences from one dataset** (exec summary vs. operational drill-down)
- Translating raw transactional data into **decision-ready KPIs**
- Applying **visual hierarchy and executive-dashboard design principles**, not just default chart placement
